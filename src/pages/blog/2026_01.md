# Too fast

It's been a bit under 4 years that ChatGPT went into the public. Many things have happened, but
there's been one constant. Everything has been moving so fast...new models, new players entering
the scene, images, videos...and in the middle of all that, layoffs, clients wanting to cut costs
and an uncertain future for all of us.

Yet, some people know it all, or so they say. They know how many less professionals will be needed,
they know what model you should be using today for each task (it came out yesterday, by the way),
they now everything. You need to stay in the loop, they say.

In case you, my friend reading this, needed a reminder: they're probably trying to sell you
something. It's ok if you haven't rushed to try Claude Fable, ChatGPT Sol or whatever frontier
model is out today. You'll try them eventually, or they will be outdated by the next thing.
Whatver.

It's not you, everything is just moving too fast.
